Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026.

Information used

CurioScope Lab Bot uses the YouTube authorization granted by the account owner to upload and manage videos on the owner’s selected YouTube channel. It stores the authorization credentials and the video metadata needed to operate that publishing workflow.

How information is used

This information is used only to create, upload, and manage CurioScope Lab Bot videos. It is not sold, rented, or used for advertising.

Sharing and retention

Information is shared with YouTube only as needed to perform the requested YouTube publishing actions. Credentials are retained until the owner revokes access or removes the service configuration.

Your choices

The owner may revoke the app’s YouTube access through their Google Account security settings. Questions or deletion requests can be sent to sanil.shrestha.12@gmail.com.
