Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Data collected

The publisher stores the TikTok account authorization token, TikTok open identifier, selected post settings, publication status, and technical logs needed to operate and troubleshoot the service.

How data is used

Data is used only to authenticate the creator, present current TikTok posting options, upload creator-approved videos, refresh authorization, and display publication status.

Storage and sharing

Application data is stored on the creator's homeserver. It is sent only to TikTok as necessary to provide the requested publishing functionality. It is not sold or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Authorization can be revoked in TikTok. Local tokens and publication history may be deleted by the creator at any time.

Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to rankedrandomdailycreator@gmail.com.
