Creator publishing workspace

Ranked Random Daily Publisher

A creator-controlled web service for reviewing and publishing Ranked Random Daily short-form videos to TikTok.

Every post is reviewed by the account owner before it is sent. The service is designed for original, licensed, or otherwise authorized video clips.

Built for deliberate publishing

  • Preview each completed video
  • Set caption, visibility, and interactions
  • Require explicit creator consent
  • Track TikTok publication status

How it works

A clear review step before every post

  1. Connect TikTokThe account owner signs in with TikTok through TikTok Login Kit.
  2. Review a generated videoThe dashboard presents the full video, caption, privacy level, and interaction settings.
  3. Give explicit consentThe creator checks a consent box before a Direct Post is created.
  4. Publish and confirmThe service transfers the approved file to TikTok and reports the resulting status.

Creator control

Nothing posts silently.

The dashboard does not schedule or silently publish videos. A creator must sign in, open a specific video, review its details, and expressly consent to that individual upload.

Data handling

Purpose-limited data

The service stores authorization and publication information only to operate the requested creator workflow. It does not sell personal data or use it for advertising.
